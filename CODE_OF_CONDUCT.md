# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be direct, be specific, and keep it about the work. Technical disagreement
is welcome and is the point of a review; personal attacks, harassment and
sustained disruption are not.

Concretely:

1. Critique the reconciliation logic, not the person who wrote it.
2. Assume the other person hit a real problem in a real close.
3. "This is wrong because X" beats "this is wrong". Bring the counterexample.
4. No harassment, discriminatory language, or unwelcome personal attention.
5. Do not publish anyone's private information, and do not post client data.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
associated with this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are handled
privately. Responses range from a warning to a permanent ban from the
project, at the maintainer's discretion.

This is a small project maintained by one person. Expect a human response
time, not an SLA.
